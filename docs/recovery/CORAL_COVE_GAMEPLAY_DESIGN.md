# Coral Cove Gameplay Design & Experience Specification

## 1. Core Experience Narrative

Coral Cove is a vibrant coral reef where children learn counting and marine biology through exploration and ecological problem-solving.

### The Adventure Loop
1. **Explore:** The child swims through the coral reef, tapping anemones, schools of fish, and seagrass patches.
2. **Encounter:** The child discovers a Green Sea Turtle searching for food. Tala the Turtle guides the observation.
3. **Understand:** The child learns real biological facts (e.g., green sea turtles eat seagrass and swim with flippers).
4. **Action (Counting):** The turtle needs a specific number of seagrass snacks. The child gathers individual seagrass items from the reef.
5. **Physical Manipulation (Kecrekan):** The child uses the Kecrekan bead frame, where one bead corresponds to one gathered snack.
6. **Reward & Progression:** Feeding the turtle restores energy to the reef, unlocks journal discovery entries, and reveals a path to Shell Shore.

---

## 2. Interaction Guidelines

- **Zero-Text Instruction:** Narration and visual cues replace reading.
- **Direct Object Tracking:** Tapping an object collects that specific item.
- **Guided Demonstration:** Tala demonstrates moving the first bead; the child continues independently.
- **Forgiving Feedback:** Incorrect counts trigger supportive hints without punishment.
