# Math Ocean — Product Vision & Design Specification

## 1. Product Identity & Core Vision

Math Ocean transforms early childhood mathematics into an immersive underwater discovery adventure. Children do not open an educational app to complete drills; they dive into a living ocean to explore coral reefs, sandy shores, and kelp forests, meeting marine creatures, solving ecological problems through natural counting and arithmetic, and discovering the wonders of the sea.

## 2. Intended Audience & Learning Objectives

- **Target Age:** 4–7 years old.
- **Learning Pillars:**
  1. **Mathematics:** Counting, one-to-one correspondence, addition, subtraction, and Jarimatika (finger-based arithmetic with ones and tens).
  2. **Marine Biology:** Real-world animal names, habitats, diets, behaviors, and ecological relationships (e.g., clownfish and sea anemones).
  3. **Environmental Stewardship:** Understanding how habitats work, why clean environments matter, and how creatures depend on each other.

## 3. Experience Principles

- **Play Before Instruction:** Mechanics are discovered through interaction, not text.
- **Demonstration Before Action:** Tala the Turtle demonstrates unfamiliar actions in context.
- **Concrete Before Abstract:** Physical manipulation of beads (Kecrekan) and fingers (Jarimatika) precedes numerical digits.
- **Exploration over Menus:** The world map is an interactive playground, not a dashboard of selectable cards.

---

## 4. The Underwater World & World Map

The world map is the central hub of Math Ocean, featuring four interconnected destinations:

1. **Coral Cove (Counting & Reef Biology):**
   - Vibrant coral reef environment inhabited by clownfish, sea turtles, and anemones.
   - Focus: Counting, number recognition, one-to-one correspondence using the Kecrekan.
2. **Shell Shore (Addition & Coastal Life):**
   - Sandy seabed and tide pools inhabited by hermit crabs and sea stars.
   - Focus: Combining groups, addition, and scavenging behaviors.
3. **Kelp Forest (Subtraction & Forest Ecology):**
   - Towering giant kelp stalks inhabited by seahorses and small schooling fish.
   - Focus: Subtraction, removal, and shelter dynamics.
4. **Finger Reef (Jarimatika Sanctuary):**
   - A tranquil sanctuary for mastering finger arithmetic (right hand = ones, left hand = tens).

---

## 5. Marine Creature Discovery System

- **Encounters:** Creatures appear naturally in their proper habitats. Tapping a creature triggers gentle observation animations and audio narration.
- **Discovery Journal:** A visual collection book tracking discovered animals, their habitats, diets, and fun scientific facts.
- **Scientific Accuracy:** Rigorously vetted biological facts ensuring children learn real marine science.

---

## 6. Teaching Methodology & Interaction Design

- **In-World Demonstration Pattern:**
  1. *Discover:* Child encounters a creature in need or an interactive puzzle.
  2. *Demonstrate:* Tala the Sea Turtle shows how to interact (e.g., sliding a Kecrekan bead).
  3. *Play:* Child performs the action.
  4. *Feedback:* Immediate multi-sensory response (bubbles, sound effect, creature reaction).

---

## 7. Gameplay Loop & Player Agency

- Exploration → Encounter → Ecological Problem → Mathematical Adventure (Kecrekan / Jarimatika) → Celebration & Reward → Habitat Restoration / New Discovery.
- No punitive failures; guidance scales dynamically when children hesitate.
